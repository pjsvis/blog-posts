### 1. The Operational Contradiction: Hijacking Jameson to Shill Julius Evola

The central premise of Jameson’s *Postmodernism, or, the Cultural Logic of Late Capitalism* [[01:11](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=aXUoodduwcs&t=71)] is systemic: postmodern pastiche, depthlessness, and the loss of historicity aren't moral failings. They are structural byproducts of multinational, financialized capital. Jameson explicitly states you cannot solve this by retreating into historical re-enactment, because that very retreat *is* the logic of the pastiche.

What does AA do?

* Around [[43:11](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=aXUoodduwcs&t=2591)], he takes Jameson’s structural demand for **“cognitive mapping”** (a class-conscious way for individuals to map their place inside global capitalism's totality).
* He maps it straight onto **Julius Evola** [[44:16](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=aXUoodduwcs&t=2656)] and neo-pagan traditionalism.
* The irony is absolute: seeking refuge in romanticized "traditionalist roots," "authentic folk continuity," or aesthetic spiritualism is **literally the exact "nostalgia mode" Jameson warned about**. It’s a simulacrum of an imagined pastoral Eden—peak *Guardians of Arcadia* LARP, mistaking an ideological escape hatch for systemic critique.
