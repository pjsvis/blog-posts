### 3\. The Practitioner Fallacy: Conflating Route with Destination

Your breakdown of the **practitioner fallacy** is a fatal blind spot in modern rehabilitation and fitness culture.

1.  **The Route:** Required acute rest, gentle mobilization, forced excess calories, temporary compensations, and micro-adaptations tailored to specific, fleeting bottlenecks.

2.  **The Destination:** A new, stable equilibrium where high-intensity loading, leaner maintenance diets, and aggressive functional movements are now tolerated.

Because of somatic amnesia and retrospective narrative bias, the practitioner completely forgets the messy, intermediate phases that kept the chassis from snapping. They look only at their current protocol and declare: _"To get where I am, just do what I do today."_

Prescribing destination-state behaviors to a damaged system in acute transit is actively harmful. Forcing high-intensity protocols on an un-stabilized, de-tensioned GLP-1 patient because "that's what fit people do" ignores the reality that their tissues have not walked the necessary prerequisite path.

### 4\. Navigating Non-Deterministic State Space

In an open, non-linear system, you cannot draw a rigid, deterministic GPS route from State A (sarcopenic crash) to State B (functional restoration). Every intervention changes the terrain itself.

The only way to navigate such a system isn't with a fixed script; it requires **dynamic, continuous feedback**:

-   **Probe and Sense:** Apply a mild mechanical or nutritional stimulus (e.g., reintroducing steady protein and low-amplitude ground loading).

-   **Observe the System's Drift:** Did the nervous system relax, or did it lock down harder? Did joints swell, or did fascial glide improve?

-   **Establish Safe Baselines First:** Just like "bed, soup, and bread," you don't ask the system to perform complex navigation while it is still reeling from the primary insult. You re-establish metabolic safety and structural invariants before nudging it anywhere else.

Assuming an unguided individual will stumble their way through that non-linear state space without supervision, while their own sensory apparatus is actively blunted by somatic amnesia, is pure optimism. Most won't find the exit; they will simply accept a lower baseline of physical resilience as their new normal.
