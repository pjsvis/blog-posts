#### Primary Pathophysiological Drivers

1. **Sarcopenic Cable Slackening:** Muscle tissue provides the variable-tension winches of the biotensegrity net. Sarcopenic lean mass loss directly depresses resting network tension ($\sigma_0$).
2. **Extracellular Matrix (ECM) Starvation:** Reduced caloric intake depresses the amino acid pool (glycine, proline, hydroxyproline) and cellular hydration required for fibroblast collagen and hyaluronan synthesis, causing tissue embrittlement and loss of inter-fascial glide.
3. **Articular Shear Localization:** When the tension network goes slack, kinetic energy generated during ambulation is no longer dissipated globally. Loads collapse straight into joint capsules, producing:
* Patellofemoral tracking errors ("sore knees").
* Pelvic sling instability and trochanteric bursitis ("hip drop").
* Plantar aponeurosis strain (foot arch collapse).


4. **Cortical Smudging ($S1$ Mapping Error):** Fascia contains roughly six times more mechanoreceptors than red muscle. As resting tension collapses, afferent signals to the primary somatosensory cortex ($S1$) degrade. The brain loses spatial resolution of the joint and responds with protective, high-tone muscular micro-bracing—experienced by personnel as a diffuse, migrating ache.

#### Phase 1: The Active Structural Defense (Target Mass Descent)

* **High-Intensity Tensile Signaling:** Perform heavy multi-planar compound lifts ($75\text{–}85\%\text{ 1RM}$) to provide the mechanotransductive strain required to signal osteoblasts and fibroblasts to preserve structural density.
* **Substrate Saturation:** Maintain a non-negotiable protein intake floor of $\ge 1.6\text{ g/kg/day}$. Ingest $15\text{–}20\text{ g}$ of hydrolyzed collagen with Vitamin C 45 minutes prior to mechanical loading.
* **Planar Sensory Reset (Floor Lying):** Conduct 15–20 minutes of daily supine floor lying (Constructive Rest). Removing the 1g vertical balance calculation eliminates defensive motor guarding, promotes viscoelastic creep of the posterior fascial sheet, and provides $S1$ with an objective planar reference to resolve cortical smudging.

#### Phase 2: The Mechanical Hand-Off (Micro-Tapering)

* **Step-Down Titration:** Avoid abrupt cessation, which triggers a severe ghrelin/leptin rebound. De-escalate dosage while extending administration intervals (7 days $\rightarrow$ 10 days $\rightarrow$ 14 days $\rightarrow$ 21 days) over 12 to 24 weeks.
* **Volumetric Mechanical Stretch:** Transition from pharmacological gastric slowing to physical gastric mechanoreceptor stimulation using high-viscosity soluble fibers and structured fluid pre-loading.

#### Phase 3: Autonomous Post-GLP Baseline

* Personnel exit the intervention with lower static mass, preserved resting tension ($\sigma_0$), clear joint tracking, and autonomous neurobehavioral control supported by conscious tracking protocols.
