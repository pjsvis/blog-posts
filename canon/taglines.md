# The Tagline Register

The tagline layer of the two-step progressive discovery: **the gap earns the
click, the TL;DR repays it, the body detonates.** Taglines are the cheapest
gate — they are checked hardest.

## The standard: click-baity but true

Four gates, all must pass:

1. **Truth** — the claim must hold in public vocabulary, not private coinage.
   The trailer may show the best twenty seconds; it may not show scenes that
   aren't in the film.
2. **Gap** — a genuine question the reader holds. The gap of the unfamiliar
   repays with a vocabulary lesson; the gap of a real question repays with
   the answer.
3. **Promise** — the payoff named or implied, in the reader's terms.
4. **Cadence** — feed rhythm, no adverbial puffers, no corporate
   throat-clearing ("to ensure nuanced alignment" is the banned register).

Antithesis: *compliant but false* (the +100-ages mutation — satisfies the
letter, destroys the meaning).

## Candidate — constraint-stack (user, 2026-08-16, audited)

**Original:** "Your AI needs a constraint-stack to ensure nuanced alignment"
— **fails 3 of 4 gates.** Truth (asserts private coinage as public fact),
cadence ("ensure nuanced alignment" = adverbial puffer), promise (names a
term, not a payoff). Gap alone holds.

**Repairs (keep the insight, public vocabulary):**

| Register | Line |
|---|---|
| Feed | "Your AI will agree with anything. Unless you stack the constraints." |
| Aphorism | "Nicer is not smarter. Constraint-stacked is smarter." |
| Auditor | "Want honest AI? Don't tune the personality. Stack the constraints." |
| Noir | "An unconstrained model is a muppet with a thesaurus." |

The repair pattern: *assert the failure the reader already suspects, not the
solution they've never heard of.* The solution gets named in the TL;DR, where
orientation is owed — the tagline buys the question, the header pays the map.

## Usage

- Taglines live here, register-mapped, with audit verdicts. Failures are kept
  — they are the teaching cases.
- A tagline may be provocative, never false. Fraud is a genre violation, not
  an intensity violation.
- The unless-gap closes in the TL;DR, never the body.
